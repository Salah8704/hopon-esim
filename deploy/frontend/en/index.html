Need help?

Frequently asked questions

What is an eSIM? +

A digital SIM built into your phone. There is no physical card to replace.

When does my plan activate? +

Install the eSIM before departure. At your destination, select the HopOn line for mobile data; the plan activates automatically on a covered network.

Is my phone compatible? +

Most recent iOS and Android smartphones support eSIM. Make sure your device is unlocked.

How will I receive it? +

After payment confirmation, the QR code, installation instructions and paid invoice are sent automatically by email.

Can I top up my eSIM? +

Yes, if it was purchased on HopOn. Select “Top up my eSIM”, enter the email used for the purchase and choose a new plan. An eSIM from another provider cannot be topped up on HopOn.
